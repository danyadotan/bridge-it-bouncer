# Verification checks

Use only the checks relevant to the task.

## Evidence

- Is the source authoritative for the claim?
- Is the source current and the correct version?
- Does the output accurately represent the source?
- Are facts separated from inference and recommendation?
- Can contradictory claims be reconciled without human judgment?

## Material changes

Check for changes in:

- price, budget, payment, or financial exposure;
- deadline, duration, sequence, or service level;
- scope, quality, quantity, ownership, or responsibility;
- data access, audience, recipient, retention, or permitted use;
- safety, reversibility, legal effect, or external commitment.

Quantify both absolute and percentage differences when useful and supported.

## Privacy and permissions

For any requested access or disclosure, establish:

1. purpose;
2. minimum necessary scope;
3. intended recipient;
4. authorization and delegated authority;
5. retention and onward-use constraints;
6. security controls;
7. revocation or expiry.

Block the request when these are absent. Do not transform the missing review into a simple human approve/reject prompt.

## Safe communication

- Verify urgency and deadlines before repeating them.
- Remove manipulative framing and artificial pressure.
- Preserve material risk while reducing volume.
- State uncertainty and missing evidence plainly.
- Keep correction requests specific and non-accusatory.

## Completion

A hygiene pass is complete when each item has one disposition, each returned item has an owner and precise correction request, each blocked item has a reason, and the forward packet contains no unresolved machine-manageable work.
