# Privacy Policy — Bridge-iT Bouncer

Effective date: October 10, 2026

## Current version

Bridge-iT Bouncer version 0.3.0 is a skills-only plugin. It does not include an MCP server, a publisher-operated API, analytics, advertising, or an external data store.

## Data handled by the plugin

The plugin processes the text and files that a user chooses to provide in the ChatGPT or Codex conversation. Bridge-iT Bouncer does not independently transmit that content to Bridge-iT or store a separate copy on Bridge-iT infrastructure.

Conversation content remains subject to the data practices, controls, and terms of the OpenAI product and workspace in which the plugin is used. Tools or services separately enabled by a user are governed by their own permissions and privacy terms.

## Data minimization

The plugin is designed to remove unnecessary personal information from downstream summaries and to block requests for personal or confidential data when purpose, scope, recipient, authorization, and minimum necessary access have not been established.

Users should not provide personal, confidential, or regulated information unless they are authorized to do so and the selected ChatGPT or Codex environment is appropriate for that information.

## Sharing, sale, and retention

Bridge-iT does not receive, sell, share, or independently retain user conversation content through this skills-only release.

## Changes

If a future release adds an MCP server, external service, telemetry, or publisher-operated storage, this policy will be updated before that capability is released publicly.

## Contact

For privacy questions, open a support request through the repository's [Support page](SUPPORT.md).
