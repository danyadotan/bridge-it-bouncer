# Support — Bridge-iT Bouncer

For installation problems, incorrect routing, privacy concerns, or reproducible behavior issues, open a GitHub issue:

https://github.com/danyadotan/bridge-it-bouncer/issues

Please include:

- the plugin version;
- the ChatGPT or Codex surface used;
- a minimal, anonymized example;
- the expected disposition and the observed disposition.

Do not include secrets, customer data, private contact details, access tokens, or confidential organizational material.
